# Out of Scope

> **Status:** Approved
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-06

## Purpose

List what is explicitly NOT part of the current MVP. These items belong to the long-term platform vision and are documented as **future scope only**. Do not implement, partially build, or design for them.

## Future Scope (not in the MVP)

### Mobile and external users

- Android application
- iOS application
- Pilgrim application (تطبيق الحجاج)
- Pilgrim management (إدارة الحجاج)

### Operations domains

- Transportation and fleet management
- GPS bus tracking
- Accommodation and camps (الإسكان والمخيمات)
- Procurement and suppliers (automatic supplier purchasing included)
- Financial management / accounting
- Emergency and crisis management
- Full Command and Control Center

### Intelligence and integrations

- Artificial intelligence
- Predictive analytics
- Advanced KPI engine
- Ministry of Hajj integrations
- Government integrations
- Smart GPS wristbands
- Health monitoring

### Platform capabilities

- Full offline mode
- Full dynamic form builder
- Full dynamic module builder
- Advanced project management
- Complaints and suggestions (الشكاوى والاقتراحات)
- Knowledge management
- Policies and procedures module
- Full seasonal cloning automation (unless approved later)

## Rules

- Any item above requires an approved **Change Request** before any design or code work.
- Never describe these items as part of the current MVP in any document.
- The API should not be shaped speculatively around these features.
